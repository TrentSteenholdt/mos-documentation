    Update Email Address

    Access Level

    Signed-in users. Accounts whose sign-in details are managed by Microsoft or Google may not be able to change their primary email here; MOS shows a message for those accounts.

    Your primary email is your MOS sign-in and password-recovery address. A secondary email is optional and receives copies of eligible notifications; it cannot be used to sign in or receive your password reset link.

    Open My Account → Update email address.

    Request a primary email change

    1. Review Current email address.
    2. Enter the proposed address in New email address and select Continue.
    3. In Verify your new email address, check the address carefully and select Send verification email.
    4. If Sign in again appears, complete sign-in, return to this page and enter the proposed address again.
    5. Open the verification email sent to the new address.

    The new address must be valid and not already used by another MOS account. Your current sign-in address stays active until you confirm the verification link. No current-password confirmation field is used; MOS may ask you to sign in again instead.

    Verify and confirm the new address

    1. Open the verification link within one hour of it being sent.
    2. Stay signed in to the same MOS account that requested the change. If prompted, sign in again.
    3. Select Verify email address.
    4. In Confirm your email change, select Confirm email change.
    5. When MOS confirms the change, sign in using the new address.

    The verification link is single-use. Requesting another verification email replaces the earlier pending request; use the latest email. If a link is expired or invalid, request a new one from Update email address.

    MOS sends a change notification to both the old and new primary addresses. Older MOS sign-in sessions end after the change, including sessions on other devices. A secondary email is left unchanged.

    An unexpected email change

    If you receive a change notification for a request you did not make, contact your association administrator promptly. Do not share verification links.

    Add, change or remove a secondary email

    The example address is fictional.

    1. Go to Update your secondary email address.
    2. Enter a valid address, or clear the field to remove it.
    3. Select Save.

    A secondary-address change is saved immediately without the primary-email verification flow. It does not change your sign-in or recovery address. Password reset links, email verification links and primary-address change alerts go to their intended account addresses rather than being copied to the secondary address.

    Troubleshooting

    • Address already in use: check the spelling and use an address that is not attached to another MOS account. Ask your administrator for help with duplicate accounts.
    • Verification email missing: check junk or spam and confirm the proposed address. Request a replacement if necessary, then use the latest link.
    • Wrong account signed in: sign out and sign in to the MOS account that requested the change, then reopen its verification email.
    • Sign-in requested again: complete sign-in and follow the returning page. When making a new request, re-enter your proposed address after signing in.
    • Link lost after navigating away: reopen the original verification email while it is still valid. If it has expired, request a new one.
    • Primary address cannot be changed here: follow MOS's provider message and manage the external account with its provider.
