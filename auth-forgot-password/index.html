    Forgot Password: Recover Your Account

    Access Level

    Available before signing in. If you use Microsoft or Google to sign in, manage that provider's password with the provider.

    Request a reset link

    1. On the sign-in page, select Forgot your password?
    2. On Reset your password, enter your MOS sign-in Email address.
    3. Select Send reset link.
    4. Check that email inbox, including junk or spam, for a MOS password reset message.

    MOS shows the same acknowledgement whether or not the address belongs to an eligible account. The acknowledgement does not confirm that an account exists or that an email was delivered. Your existing password does not change when you request a link.

    The email contains a link to choose a password, not a temporary password. The link expires one hour after it was requested and can be used once. Do not forward it or share it with anyone.

    Choose your new password

    1. Open the link in the reset email.
    2. On Choose a new password, enter New password.
    3. Enter it again in Confirm new password.
    4. Select Change password.
    5. When MOS confirms the change, return to sign-in and use your new password.

    Your new password must be 8–64 characters and contain at least three of these four types: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. Use a unique password and save it in a password manager.

    Older MOS sign-in sessions end after a successful reset. Sign in again on any other devices you use.

    If the link does not work

    • Expired or already used: select Request a new link and repeat the request. A successful reset link cannot be reused.
    • Incomplete link or a page refreshed before completion: reopen the original email link, or request a new one. Use the complete link from the email.
    • The password change could not be completed: request a new link and try again. If the problem continues, contact your association administrator.
    • Too many requests: wait before trying again. Repeated requests are limited.

    If the email does not arrive

    1. Check junk or spam and allow time for delivery.
    2. Make sure you requested the link for your primary sign-in address, not a secondary notification address.
    3. If you normally select Microsoft or Google at sign-in, use that provider's recovery options.
    4. Ask your association administrator to confirm your account details or request a recovery link for you.

    Passwords are no longer recovered by answering a secret question. If you have lost access to your sign-in email, contact your administrator for help; a saved secret answer does not replace access to that email.
